Connect only a TikTok account you own or are authorized to use. Review the selected file, captions, description, and destination account before approving an upload. Keep your TikTok developer credentials and local project files private.

TikTok uploads and publication

Studio uses TikTok OAuth to connect your account. Each upload requires your explicit approval in Studio. The approved video goes to your TikTok inbox for further review. You choose the final description and privacy options in TikTok and decide whether to publish. A notification that a clip reached the inbox indicates that it is available for review. An operation marked PUBLISH_COMPLETE reflects the status reported by TikTok.

TikTok controls account eligibility, available privacy options, API permissions, review, and publication. Your use of TikTok is subject to its terms and policies. Use Studio’s Disconnect control or your TikTok account settings to revoke access. See the Privacy Policy for details on local files and history.
